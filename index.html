Try the tools. Paste something and watch.

Paste any messy text. Graze pulls out the emails, phone numbers, dates, amounts and links.

Paste emails, with a blank line between each one. Graze sorts them and suggests what to do next.

Paste the steps you do every day, one per line. Graze finds the part that repeats and shows how to automate it.

Paste a table with a header row, with commas between columns. Graze summarizes every column and charts the biggest groups.

Have a Nebius or NVIDIA key? Test live answers

Paste your own key to get real Nemotron answers. It stays in this browser tab (session storage) and is sent only with your request, through this site's function to Nebius Token Factory, or to NVIDIA if the key starts with nvapi-. It is never saved or logged. Close the tab to forget it.

The rule based answer appears instantly in your browser. When live AI is on, an open model adds its own answer below, with the model used and the estimated cost.
